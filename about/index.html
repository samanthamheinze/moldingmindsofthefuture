MY STORY

It started with my own students

I kept creating resources for my students that were fun, engaging, and made a real impact in my classroom, and I wanted to share them. Today I focus on unique learning resources and experiences that are inquiry based and learner centered, and that save teachers time and effort.

Along the way, I kept hearing the same thing from parents. They wanted to help their kids at home but didn't know how. They weren't missing love or effort, they were missing a plan. That's why I create simple activities and guides for families.
