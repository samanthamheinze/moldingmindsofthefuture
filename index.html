The Person Behind the Tools

Hi, I'm Samantha 👋

I'm an elementary educator with a deep passion for making math accessible to every K-5 student. After years in classrooms and tutoring sessions, I kept running into the same problem: there was no quick, reliable way to figure out exactly where a struggling student was falling behind.

So I built one. And then another. Molding Minds of the Future is my collection of free tools and printable resources — the same ones I use with real students, and the ones I wish I'd had on day one.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

The K-5 Math Diagnostic is a free, browser-based assessment that identifies exactly which math skills a student has mastered and where gaps exist — covering every major standard from Kindergarten through Grade 5. It generates a personalized grade-level placement and a downloadable PDF report so you know exactly where to start teaching.
Yes — both the K-5 Math Diagnostic and Grocery Store Math tool are free to open and use in any browser. No login, no download needed. The Grocery Store tool also has a companion Activity Guide on Teachers Pay Teachers with structured lesson plans and activities to use alongside it.
All tools are designed for Kindergarten through Grade 5 (K-5). The Math Diagnostic covers every standard in this range. Grocery Store Math works best for students in grades 2 and up who are comfortable with basic addition and subtraction.
Open the Math Diagnostic on any device with a browser — no setup needed. Have the student enter their name and grade level, then work through the questions. A hand-raise prompt appears when teacher assistance is needed. At the end, download the PDF report showing their grade-level placement and recommended starting point.
Printable worksheets, activity packs, and lesson bundles are available in Samantha's Teachers Pay Teachers store. Browse and purchase directly at teacherspayteachers.com/store/molding-minds-of-the-future — many resources are free or low-cost.
