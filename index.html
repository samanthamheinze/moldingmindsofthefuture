K-5 Math Diagnostic
Assessment

This isn't a worksheet with a score at the bottom. It's an adaptive online assessment that pinpoints exactly where each student should start, standard by standard, across five math domains independently. A student can be ahead in one area and still building in another, and this tool finds both.

  • Adapts up or down based on student responses
  • Grade-level placement for every domain
  • Every mastered standard listed individually
  • Recommended starting point per domain
  • Ready-to-share parent note in plain language
  • Full question-by-question log
  • Downloadable PDF report
  • Progress saves automatically

The Person Behind the Tools

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

The K-5 Math Diagnostic is an adaptive, browser-based assessment that identifies exactly which math skills a student has mastered and where gaps exist, covering every major standard from Kindergarten through Grade 5. It generates a personalized grade-level placement and a downloadable PDF report so you know exactly where to start teaching. Available for purchase on Teachers Pay Teachers.
Your purchase on Teachers Pay Teachers includes the online assessment access link, a full how-to guide, and five Teaching Ideas and Example Questions guides, one for each math domain. The Grocery Store Math tool on this site is free to use in any browser, and also has a companion Activity Guide on TPT.
The interactive tools cover K-5 math skills. The curriculum resources on Teachers Pay Teachers span a wider range, including elementary (K-5), upper elementary (3-6), and middle school (6-8) resources across math, SEL, ELA, and life skills.
After purchasing on Teachers Pay Teachers, you receive the access link and a complete how-to guide. The student enters their name and grade level, then works through the adaptive questions. A hand-raise prompt appears when the tutor or teacher needs to step in. At the end, download the PDF report showing their grade-level placement and recommended starting point per domain.
Printable worksheets, activity packs, and lesson bundles are available in Samantha's Teachers Pay Teachers store. Browse and purchase directly at teacherspayteachers.com/store/molding-minds-of-the-future. Resources cover math, SEL, ELA, financial literacy, and more.
