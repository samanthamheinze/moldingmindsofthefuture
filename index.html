K-5 Math Diagnostic
Assessment

This isn't a worksheet with a score at the bottom. It's an adaptive online assessment that pinpoints exactly where each student should start, standard by standard, across five math domains independently. A student can be ahead in one area and still building in another, and this tool finds both.

  • Adapts up or down based on student responses
  • Grade-level placement for every domain
  • Every mastered standard listed individually
  • Recommended starting point per domain
  • Ready-to-share parent note in plain language
  • Full question-by-question log
  • Downloadable PDF report
  • Progress saves automatically

The Person Behind the Tools

Hi, I'm Samantha 👋

I'm an educator with a deep passion for making learning accessible to every student. After years in classrooms and tutoring sessions, I kept running into the same problem: there was no quick, reliable way to figure out exactly where a struggling student was falling behind.

So I built one. And then another. Molding Minds of the Future is my collection of tools and curriculum resources, the same ones I use with real students and the ones I wish I'd had on day one.
