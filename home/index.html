WHAT I BELIEVE

Every child can be an inquisitive learner

Whether it's a classroom of 25 or the kitchen table, kids learn best when they're curious, having fun, and asked to explain their thinking.

Curiosity first
Questions like "why?" and "how do you know?" turn every activity into deeper thinking.
Learning through play
Games, safaris, quests, and real world projects make skills stick.
Meet them where they are
Find each child's starting point, then celebrate progress, not perfection.
School and home, together
When teachers and families work together, kids grow faster and feel more confident.

Sight Word Movement Cards

Each letter is a movement, so kids spell and act out 179 Dolch sight words for K, 1st, and 2nd grade. A perfect brain break in class and a fun way to practice at home.

Two sets of cards: one with the moves pictured, and one with just the word. Print the Alphabet Movement Chart as a poster, and kids can look at it to do the poses on their own.
